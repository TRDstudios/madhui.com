The Ethos of Madhui Infratech

In the present competitive and dynamic real estate sector, very few players have such an enviable track record as Madhui Infratech, which sets high standards in the building industry through architectural integrity, technological capability, constant innovation, and flawless execution. We create landmarks across commercial and residential sectors, guided by an unwavering commitment to customer trust, transparency, and timely delivery.

Madhui Infratech is part of the Madhui Group, a prominent business conglomerate established in 1974. With a well-diversified portfolio and strong business ethics, the group is led by experienced professionals adopting modern technology, consumer research, and employee-friendly policies.

Mission

To build landmarks by crafting innovative, sustainable and high quality real estate solutions that inspire and delight customers and create economic value.

Vision

To be a fast-growing business brand, transforming the business real estate landscape, through cutting-edge technologies and impeccable standards of quality to set new benchmarks for the industry.

Integrity

Integrity builds transparency, trust and safeguards our corporate reputation.

Accountability

Individuals take ownership of their decisions and business outcomes.

Customer Centricity

Deliver high quality, innovative products for customer delight.

Social Responsibility

Creating a sustainable community ensuring a better future for all stakeholders.

Innovation

Challenging norms, embracing tech and operational efficiency.

Excellence

High quality, service and meticulous attention to detail.

Leadership

MR. M Prabhakar Rao

Chairman & Managing Director

Mr M Prabhakar Rao, the Chairman and Managing Director of the Madhui Group is a Gold Medalist from Banaras Hindu University in M.Sc. (Agri) and stood first during his graduation. Since he joined the Company, he played key leadership roles across the group, guiding expansion plans into new segments like Power, Textiles, Sugars, Infrastructure and Real Estate. Thanks to his sound knowledge base and business acumen, Nuziveedu Seeds is now the largest privately held seed company in India with a major customer base. In recognition of his contributions, he has been elected President of the National Seed Association of India (NSAI), Chairman of the Cotton Committee of NSAI, and Governing Board Member of central agriculture research universities. Under his aegis, seed and infrastructure operations have been awarded national accolades for corporate development by the Government of India and various central ministries.

Aishwarya M.

Executive Director

Mrs. M. Aishwarya, Executive Director of Madhui Infratech, is in the form of our core operating and administrative lead, driving innovation, growth and success. Mrs. Aishwarya holds a master's degree in business administration from University of Cambridge, United Kingdom with experience in investment banking sector. As an Executive Director, she sets the strategic direction for commercial real estate ventures. Her visionary leadership has been instrumental in the organization's ability to identify emerging trends, capitalize on new market opportunities and consistently deliver outstanding results. Her commitment to excellence coupled with deep knowledge of the industry has earned her a distinguished reputation as a leader in the field. Mrs. Aishwarya believes in the power of collaborative environments. Her leadership style promotes an environment where creativity and innovation flourish. Her comprehensive understanding of the real estate industry, ability to navigate complex transactions, negotiate and close deals has been instrumental in our continuous rise.

Cdr. Vinod Nathan (Retd.)

Chief Operating Officer

Cdr. Vinod Nathan (Retd.) joined the company in October 2023 after working for 22 years with the Indian Navy. An electrical engineer holding masters in nuclear engineering & technology (IIT, Bombay) and certificate course in Management (IIM, Lucknow), he brings with him the organizational capabilities imbibed from the Navy. He has handled numerous projects from conceptualization to execution in the past and is responsible for the management of all timely and joint developments of the Company.
