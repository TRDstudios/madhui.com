About NSL Group

Nuziveedu Seeds Limited was established in mid '70s with the business objective of providing high quality seeds and to contribute to the agricultural economy of the country. Thereafter, the Company diversified strategically into a variety of businesses.

Presently, the NSL Group is led by Mandava Holdings Private Limited, the Holding Company. NSL Group has diversified into various business sectors such as infrastructure, power, textiles, sugars apart from the flagship business of seeds. Please click the link for details: www.nslgroup.co.in

Group Companies Overview

Nuziveedu Seeds Ltd

Nuziveedu Seeds Ltd is India's largest BT cotton seed company in terms of volumes of seed packets sold. It develops, produces, and distributes hybrid and varietal seed products. It is the flagship business of the NSL Group and one of the most trusted seed companies in India.

NSL Infratech

With a foundation firmly planted in its founding beliefs of architectural integrity, technological superiority, innovation, and flawless execution, NSL INFRATECH is making giant strides towards reshaping the world of infrastructure for a better tomorrow. Our ingenious approach to construction stands to last.

NSL Textiles Ltd

NSL Textiles Ltd is one of the largest and fully integrated textile companies in India, equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and promoted with the concept of "Cotton to Clothing". The manufacturing operations are spread over 8 locations and provide massive employment opportunities in the textile sector with 7,000+ employees in Andhra Pradesh.

NSL Sugars Ltd

NSL Sugars Limited (NSL) is one of the most efficient and integrated sugar companies in South India. Established as an associate company of the NSL Group in 2003-04, the organization aims to foster economic growth through agro-based industry in rural India. Today, NSL Sugars is amongst the top ten sugar companies in India with five integrated sugar complexes across Karnataka, Telangana & Maharashtra.

NSL Renewable Power Pvt Ltd

NSL Renewable Power Private Limited is one of the fastest-growing and independent renewable power companies with interests in wind farms, hydel power, and biomass plants. The company follows an unparalleled business model in each of the verticals and has evolved as an independent power producer playing a vital role in satisfying India's energy needs through innovation and sustainable methods.
