Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, under the general permission granted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), NRIs can acquire residential and commercial properties in India. However, they cannot buy agricultural land, plantation property, or farm houses.

Rental income earned from properties in India is subject to income tax. When selling property, capital gains tax is applicable. Short-term capital gains are taxed at normal slab rates, while long-term capital gains are taxed at 20% with indexation benefits.

Yes, leading banks and financial institutions in India offer home loans to NRIs for purchasing residential property. The loan can cover up to 75-80% of the property cost, subject to credit checks and income eligibility.
