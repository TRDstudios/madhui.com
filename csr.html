Focus Areas & Initiatives

Agriculture

  • Promotion of sustainable agricultural practices among marginal farmers.
  • Conducting organic composting workshops and soil health testing camps.
  • Providing custom hybrid seed aggregates to secure crop yields.
Agriculture Development

Women Empowerment

  • Vocational training programs focusing on tailoring and embroidery designs.
  • Setting up local Self Help Groups (SHGs) to manage micro-credit lines.
  • Creating digital literacy programs for village women and girl child students.
Read PR Newswire

Health

  • Operating periodic mobile health clinics in remote tribal sectors.
  • Free medicine distribution drives and cataract surgery camps.
  • Safe drinking water projects installing RO purification units.
Read CSR Health Report

Livelihood

  • Livestock vaccination programs and dairy farming support networks.
  • Creating vocational institutes focusing on plumbing, electricals, and retail skills.
  • Providing financial toolkits to youth entrepreneurs starting micro-businesses.
Livelihoods Focus Details

Education

  • Upgrading infrastructure of rural government primary schools.
  • Providing scholastic kits (bags, books, uniforms) to low-income students.
  • Sponsoring special scholarship programs for students pursuing higher studies.
Impact Site Overview

Zero Liquid Discharge - NSL Textiles

  • Deploying state-of-the-art recycling plants at all textile units.
  • Reclaiming 95%+ of processed water blocks to reuse in machinery.
  • Ensuring zero toxic effluents reach local agricultural canals.
Impact Site Overview

Lake Rejuvenation

  • Desilting and cleaning water bodies located near commercial IT complexes.
  • Planting indigenous trees around lake boundaries to prevent erosion.
  • Installing aeration fountains and bio-fencing to preserve micro-ecosystems.
Impact Site Overview
